# 📊 FinancePro: Local Expense & EMI Tracker

A sleek, privacy-focused financial dashboard designed to track expenses, manage long-term loans (EMIs), and monitor recurring subscriptions. **FinancePro** runs entirely in your browser—no database, no sign-ups, and no data leaves your machine.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏠 Integrated Dashboard
*   **Real-time Totals:** Instantly see your total expenses and income balance.
*   **Quick Post:** Add transactions with a simple description and amount.
*   **Downloadable Reports:** Generate a PDF or print your financial summary with one click using the built-in download button.

### 📊 Advanced EMI Tracker
*   **Progress Visualization:** Clean progress bars showing how much of a loan is cleared.
*   **Smart Payment:** One-click "PAID" button that automatically updates your loan balance and logs a transaction in your history.
*   **Lifetime vs. Monthly View:** Track what you've paid over the life of the loan versus your current month's commitment.

### 🔔 Subscription Management
*   **Billing Reminders:** Keep track of your monthly services and their billing dates.
*   **Spend Analysis:** See exactly how much your monthly subscriptions add up to.
*   **Dedicated Export:** Export your subscription list separately for easy auditing.

### ⚙️ Customization & Privacy
*   **Multi-Theme Support:** Toggle between Light, Dark, Glassmorphism, and Nord themes.
*   **Currency Switcher:** Full support for £ (GBP), $ (USD), € (EUR), and ₹ (INR).
*   **100% Private:** All data is saved to your browser's `localStorage`. No cloud, no tracking, no risk.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  **Download/Copy:** Save the `index.html` file to your computer.
2.  **Open:** Double-click the file to open it in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
3.  **Manage:** Start adding your expenses. Your data will persist even if you refresh or close the tab!

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

*   **HTML5:** Semantic structure for a fast, accessible UI.
*   **CSS3:** Custom properties (variables) for real-time theme switching and responsive grid layouts.
*   **JavaScript (Vanilla):** Logic for data persistence, mathematical calculations, and UI state management.
*   **LocalStorage API:** For high-speed, client-side data storage.

---

## 📋 Data Export & Reports
To export your data:
1.  Navigate to the **Dashboard**, **EMI**, or **Subscription** tab.
2.  Click the **Download (⬇️)** button located in the header card.
3.  Choose **"Save as PDF"** in your browser's print dialogue to keep a digital record.
